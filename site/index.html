The workflow

Drop. Set. Done.

Drag and drop

Drop your photos or entire folders straight onto the window. There is no drop target to aim for and no dialog to click through — anywhere in the app will do.

Pick the factor

Select your anamorphic lens’s squeeze ratio — 1.33×, 1.5×, 2× or a custom value — and choose the output format you want to end up with.

Collect your files

Processed files land in a desqueezed/ folder right next to the originals. Batches run in parallel, and nothing ever leaves your machine.

Lossless by default

Edit straight in Lightroom

In DNG mode, Desqueeze processes your images with no loss of quality at all — so you can open your anamorphic photos in Lightroom and grade them as they are, without converting them to bitmaps in Photoshop first.

It works because nothing is stretched. Desqueeze converts to DNG and records the squeeze factor in the file’s DefaultScale tag, leaving every pixel exactly where the sensor put it. Lightroom, Camera Raw and any other DNG-aware app read that tag and apply the correct proportions on the fly.

Resampling would mean interpolating the whole image onto a new grid — inventing detail, baking the stretch in permanently, and spending quality before grading has even started.

DNGLab, dcraw_emu and ExifTool all ship inside the app, so there is nothing else to install. Prefer a flat file? Export to JPEG, PNG, TIFF or WebP and Desqueeze bakes the stretch into the pixels instead, with dual-illuminant DNG profiles handling the D65→D50 chromatic adaptation.

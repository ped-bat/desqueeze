# Version 0.1.2 (3 Sep 2026)

## Interface

- **Letterbox bars.** The top and bottom bars now render as bars: a light
  backdrop blur that softens the dot grid behind them, closed off by a
  hairline edge. Previously they were declared but drew nothing, and only
  the bottom one was ever placed.
- **CRT scanlines now cover the whole interface.** They lived inside the
  canvas layer, so they were painted underneath the buttons, bars and text
  instead of across them.
- **One hover animation everywhere.** Buttons used a fast curve going in
  and a slow one coming out, so moving the pointer on and off quickly
  swapped easing mid-animation and stuttered. Hover now runs on a single
  spring — the same spring physics the dot grid and title use.
- **Steadier screen changes.** The action buttons no longer jump to their
  new position while the rest of the screen is still fading, and switching
  to or from Settings no longer flickers for a frame.
- **The settings panel keeps its width.** Choosing a shorter option used to
  shrink the panel and slide both edges inward.

## Under the hood

- Updated to Electron 44. Electron 39 had reached end of life and was no
  longer receiving Chromium security patches.
- `npm audit` now reports zero vulnerabilities, down from 47 advisories.
  The one that shipped in the app was an argument-injection issue in
  exiftool-vendored (GHSA-cw26-7653-2rp5); the rest were build-time only.
